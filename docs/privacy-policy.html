Privacy Policy

Currency Calc  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Currency Calc is a free, ad-free calculator app with live currency conversion. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, location, or any other identifying information.

Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your selected home and local currencies
  • Recently used currencies
  • Any custom exchange rates you have set
  • Calculation history
  • App preferences (theme, haptic feedback setting)

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

Network requests

The app fetches exchange rate data from open.er-api.com, a third-party exchange rate service. These requests contain no personal data — only a standard HTTP request for the latest USD-based rates. Please refer to the ExchangeRate-API terms of service for details on how they handle requests.

Rates are cached on your device for up to 24 hours to minimise network usage.

Third-party services

The app uses no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools. The only external service contacted is open.er-api.com as described above.

Children's privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. It contains no user accounts, social features, or data collection of any kind.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date at the top of the page.
