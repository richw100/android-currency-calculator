Details

Currency & Card Fees

Rates are fetched from open.er-api.com — 161 currencies, no API key required. Set a custom rate if you've locked one in with your bank or travel money provider.

Card profiles support a fee percentage, a minimum fee, and per-card rate overrides — so you always see exactly what a transaction will cost you.

Receipt Scanner

Point your camera at any receipt, menu, or price tag and TripCalc reads the numbers automatically using on-device text recognition. Tap any detected price to see its converted value overlaid directly on top of the original — covering the number so nothing below is obscured.

Select multiple prices to build a running total, then send it straight to the currency converter or tip calculator in one tap. Pinch to zoom for small print.

  • Converted values shown as an overlay on the original price
  • Multi-select with running total and converted equivalent
  • Auto-detects the receipt's currency from symbols (£, $, €, ¥, etc.)
  • Toggle conversion on/off — your setting is remembered
  • All image processing happens on-device — photos are never uploaded

Local Info

Look up practical travel information for over 80 countries. Select any country to instantly see emergency numbers, calling code, local currency, official languages, driving side, speed limits (mph or km/h), drink-drive BAC limit, tap water safety, and plug type + voltage.

Driving side and emergency numbers are sourced live from Wikidata (CC0). Calling code, currency and languages come from restcountries.com. All data includes a disclaimer — always verify locally before travelling.

Unit Conversions at a Glance

  • Miles ↔ Kilometres, Feet ↔ Metres, Inches ↔ Centimetres, Yards ↔ Metres
  • Square feet ↔ Square metres, Acres ↔ Hectares
  • UK/US Pint, Gallon ↔ Litres/ml, Fluid oz ↔ ml, US Cup ↔ ml
  • Stone+lb ↔ kg (compound entry), Pounds ↔ kg, Ounces ↔ g
  • mph ↔ km/h, PSI ↔ Bar, kcal ↔ kJ
  • Celsius ↔ Fahrenheit
  • MPG (UK or US) ↔ Litres per 100 km, mi/kWh ↔ kWh/100 km
