TripCalc — the calculator built for travel.

Everything you need when you're abroad, in one place. Convert currencies as you type, split a restaurant bill, convert miles to kilometres, Fahrenheit to Celsius, and check your fuel economy — all without leaving the app.


CURRENCY CONVERSION

Rates are fetched automatically from open.er-api.com, covering 161 currencies worldwide. Rates refresh every 24 hours and are cached so the app keeps working even when you're offline. Set a custom rate if you've already locked in your exchange rate from a bank or travel money provider — the app shows exactly how it compares to the live rate.


TIP & BILL SPLITTING

Enter the bill, choose your tip percentage (or set a custom one), and split it however many ways you need. One tap sends your share straight to the currency converter — so you always know what you're paying in your home currency.


UNIT CONVERSIONS

• Miles ↔ Kilometres
• Celsius ↔ Fahrenheit
• MPG (UK or US) ↔ Litres per 100km


FULL CALCULATOR

All the standard operations — add, subtract, multiply, divide — plus brackets for complex expressions and a percentage key. A scrollable history of recent calculations lets you tap any result to restore it instantly.


CUSTOMISABLE APPEARANCE

Choose from five accent colour schemes. Switch between dark mode, light mode, or follow your device's system setting.


NO ADS, EVER

Clean, distraction-free interface. No banner ads, no pop-ups, no subscriptions. Just a calculator.
