TripCalc — the calculator built for travel.

Your travel companion for money and more. Live currency conversion across 161 currencies, card fee profiles so you always know what you'll actually pay, plus a tip calculator, fuel converter, distance and temperature tools, and an international clothing and shoe size guide — everything you need when you're away from home.

No ads. No account. No tracking.


CURRENCY CONVERSION

Rates are fetched automatically from open.er-api.com, covering 161 currencies worldwide. Rates refresh every 24 hours and are cached so the app keeps working offline. Set a custom rate if you've already locked in your exchange rate from a bank or travel money provider.


CARD PROFILES

Create named profiles for your payment cards, each with its own foreign transaction fee percentage and optional minimum charge (e.g. 2.75% or minimum £3). The app applies the correct fee automatically and shows the true cost of every transaction. Add per-card exchange rate overrides for currencies where your card offers a fixed or non-live rate.


TIP & BILL SPLITTING

Enter the bill, choose your tip percentage (or set a custom one), and split it however many ways you need. One tap sends the total straight to the currency converter — so you always know what you're paying in your home currency.


UNIT CONVERSIONS

• Miles ↔ Kilometres
• Inches ↔ Centimetres
• Feet ↔ Metres
• Square feet ↔ Square metres
• Celsius ↔ Fahrenheit
• MPG (UK or US) ↔ Litres per 100km


CLOTHING & SHOE SIZE CONVERTER

Convert between international sizing standards for men's and women's clothing and shoes — covering UK, EU, US, and more. Tap a size in one system to instantly see the equivalent in another.


FULL CALCULATOR

All the standard operations — add, subtract, multiply, divide — plus brackets for complex expressions and a percentage key. A scrollable history of recent conversions lets you tap any entry to restore it instantly, with optional notes to label what each calculation was for.


CUSTOMISABLE APPEARANCE

Choose from five accent colour schemes. Switch between dark mode, light mode, or follow your device's system setting.
