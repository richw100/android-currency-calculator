Currency Calc — the calculator that converts as you type.

Most currency apps make you switch between a calculator and a converter. Currency Calc combines both in one place. Enter any amount — or a full calculation — and see the live converted equivalent update in real time alongside your working.


LIVE EXCHANGE RATES

Rates are fetched automatically from open.er-api.com, covering 161 currencies worldwide. Rates refresh every 24 hours and are cached so the app keeps working even when you're offline.


FULL CALCULATOR

All the standard operations you'd expect — add, subtract, multiply, divide — plus brackets for complex expressions and a percentage key. A scrollable history of recent calculations lets you tap any result to restore it instantly.


CUSTOM RATES

Travelling soon, or already have a locked-in rate from your bank or travel money provider? Set your own exchange rate for any currency pair. The app shows exactly how your custom rate compares to the live rate, so you always know what you're getting.


161 CURRENCIES

From major pairs like GBP/EUR and USD/JPY to lesser-known currencies. Search by name or code, and your recently used currencies appear at the top for quick access.


CUSTOMISABLE APPEARANCE

Choose from five accent colour schemes for the calculator buttons. Switch between dark mode, light mode, or follow your device's system setting. All preferences are saved and restored between sessions.


NO ADS, EVER

Clean, distraction-free interface. No banner ads, no pop-ups, no subscriptions. Just a calculator.
